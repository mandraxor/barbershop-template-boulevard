01. The Boulevard Standard

Old-World Craftsmanship.
Downtown Soul.

Located in the Historic 6th Street Commercial District at 906 S 6th St #105, Boulevard Barber Shop preserves the timeless rituals of traditional American barbering while executing modern urban styling.

Every chair is staffed by veteran artisans—Elier, Bryan, Lay, and Henry—dedicated to skin fades, textured scissor work, custom beard contours, and authentic hot lather straight razor shaves.
